# precisionenvelope 0.1.0

* First release.
* `precision_envelope()` computes the sharp envelope, retention, standard
  error inflation, phases and threshold missingness rates at the planning
  stage, for a continuous covariate space or for indivisible strata.
* `recovery_plan()` gives the minimum-cost targeted recovery rule.
* `envelope_fit()` and `envelope_bootstrap()` estimate the envelope for the
  mean of a partially observed continuous outcome, with a simultaneous
  refitted-bootstrap band and certified phases.
